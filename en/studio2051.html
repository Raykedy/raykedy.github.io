About the Project

Studio 2051 is the working platform behind “The Awakening Algorithm”, created as a collaborative space for artists, musicians, programmers, and creative, critically minded people.

The studio brings together people who create with purpose — to help make the world more aware and humane.

Collaboration

Studio 2051 welcomes collaboration with artists, musicians, programmers, developers, and independent creators from different fields. We see collaboration as building projects together — from the first idea and concept through production and continued development.

  • Books, literary works, and philosophical projects
  • Music videos, visual stories, and multimedia formats
  • Music and audiovisual projects
  • Video games, interactive systems, and applications
  • Experimental digital and hybrid media

Our priority is work created with meaning and growth in mind, rather than work shaped only by passing trends.

For Clients

Studio 2051 produces commissioned work in visual, multimedia, and advertising production. We work with brands and independent clients who value meaning, atmosphere, and a coherent final result.

  • Advertising and brand films from €15 / 10 sec

  • Visual presentations and concept films custom quote
  • Visual materials and video campaigns for books and audiobooks custom quote
  • Multimedia and interactive projects custom quote
  • Visual and conceptual development for digital products and ideas custom quote

Every project is considered individually. Format, scope, and working terms are defined during the project discussion. Flexible payment arrangements are available.
